# Mechanical CAD & product design

## Precision pump bracket
Parametric SolidWorks part modelling and A3 manufacturing drawing for an aluminium 6061-T6 bracket. The drawing includes section/detail views, threaded holes, datum references, positional tolerances and an as-machined finish with deburred edges. Calculated dowel-pin diametral interference: 0.00254–0.01262 mm; installation requires verification. Evidence: drawing and part described in the CAD portfolio, not uploaded here.

## Mechanical lock assembly
Developed and reviewed a multi-component SolidWorks model covering the case, forend, latch, follower, deadbolt, levers, springs and spacers. Considered part placement, mounting, clearances and intended movement.

## Elastic-band competition buggy
Designer and project coordinator for a geared elastic-band-powered vehicle. Brief limits: 250 × 250 × 250 mm envelope and no more than four elastic bands. Considered gear ratio, torque transfer, traction, stability and component placement. Prototype performance tests formed part of the project; numerical results are not supplied.

## MSAFIRI device enclosure
Developed a Blender casing concept and contributed to prototype leadership for a passenger-capacity monitoring and interlock concept. Focused on electronics packaging, accessible display placement and practical housing. The physical prototype does not establish certified performance or production manufacture.
