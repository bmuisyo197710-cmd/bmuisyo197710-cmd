# Vehicle systems & engineering analysis

## EV tractor battery vibration isolation
Final-year investigation into passive isolation for an electric tractor battery pack operating on rugged terrain. Work included vehicle acceleration visualisation, modelling vibration behaviour and examining stiffness, damping, natural frequency, resonance risk and transmissibility using MATLAB/Simulink. The supplied materials do not provide numerical validation results or final design parameters.

## Autonomous electric vehicle
Academic four-wheel autonomous buggy project integrating mechanical design, sensors, actuators and basic control systems through CAD, manufacturing and testing. Detailed test data and source files are not supplied.
